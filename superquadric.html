    Up axis of this model

    Your model becomes part 1, rebuilt as a distance field so joins, cuts and the shell work on it. Add superquadric parts to cut into it or join onto it. Models stay in this browser only.

    Parts

    Each part is a superquadric, or your own model as part 1. Parts after the first add to, cut from or intersect with the ones before. Up to 4.

    Click a part in the view: drag the arrows to move it, the rings to turn it. Click empty space to hide them.

    Profile

    ε₁ shapes the side profile, ε₂ the cross-section. Low is square, 1 is round, 2 is a diamond. Set top and base apart to change shape along the height.
